About Melissa

Writer Builder Former paramedic Founder

Melissa Miller is a former paramedic turned creative writer and builder. Years in emergency medicine, then customer service and sales, taught her to listen closely and find the right words when they matter.

She writes poetry, essays, copy, and scripts, and she builds the sites, products, and projects around them. ChatGPT, Claude, Canva, Picsart, and AI image and video tools are part of her everyday work, always finished by her own eye and voice.

Hidden Light Collective is the umbrella for all of it: the service work, the products, the stories, and the communities she is growing.

Writer first

Poetry, personal essays, copywriting, and scripts, in her own voice.

AI, then human

She uses AI tools daily, then shapes and finishes every piece herself.
